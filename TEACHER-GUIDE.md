# SUMMER → SEPTEMBER — Teacher Guide

## Format

- Age: 13–16
- Level: confident A2 → A2+
- Mode: one student + one tutor
- Duration: two lessons of about 60 minutes
- Simple instructions are in English; Russian appears only as optional support.

## Lesson 1 — My Summer Story

1. Mood Check — 8 min. Choose a feeling, complete a reason and ask the tutor. Face Detective, typing and Mood Mixer are optional lexical routes.
2. Travel Map — 12 min. Activate 18 travel items and build a personal travel line. Choose one of four game tabs; do not complete all four in the Core Route.
3. Keks’s Files — 14 min. Use the BBC Video Stop plus one listening file. Each file now has gist, detail, True/False/Not Mentioned, phrase hunt and transcript.
4. Language Lab — 15 min. Use guided discovery, one 10-item exercise and personal production. Verb Vault is the game route.
5. Story Quest — 12 min. Open clues, order eight genuinely shuffled events and retell. The reading text is Extra Practice.
6. Summer Reel Studio — 11 min. Choose a hook, plan cue cards, speak for 75 seconds and give delayed feedback. No upload or account is required.

Target language: Past Simple; travel vocabulary; feelings; sequence markers.
Correction priority: past forms, did/didn’t + base verb, clear sequencing.

## Lesson 2 — My September Plan

1. Prediction Wheel — 10 min. Activate adjectives and spin; typing practice and the BBC one-minute video are expandable routes.
2. Wish or Plan — 15 min. Use the new future-forms reference, one 10-item set and production. Other sets are Extra Practice.
3. Goal Lab — 12 min. Choose a school-relevant teen goal and turn it into a small action.
4. Dream Trip — 13 min. Learn 20 items, choose one vocabulary game, pack eight things and pitch the trip.
5. Plan Builder — 12 min. Learn teen-relevant habits, practise meaning/recall and choose only three.
6. Future Message — 10 min. Build a 90–120 word time capsule or voice script. Copy, print and support controls are included; the school-year reading is Extra Practice.

Target language: want to, would like to, hope, plan to, be going to, Present Continuous for arrangements, will for decisions/promises/predictions and Present Simple for timetables.
Correction priority: meaning before terminology; realistic action + time/frequency.

## 1:1 interaction

Use the visible Tutor Follow-up and Ask Your Tutor strips. Tutor notes are closed by default. In speaking tasks, do not interrupt the first attempt: note one successful feature and one target improvement.

## Reset

New student clears answers, progress, feedback, local browser storage, open hint panels, audio positions and active speech. It returns the course to Lesson 1, Mission 1. Story cards reshuffle for the next student.

## Clear audio route

Use **Play clear voice** first. The site requests a British or neutral English system voice at 0.9× speed. A local MP3 remains inside the package as an offline backup. Unlock transcripts only after the learner has attempted at least one listening answer.

## Closed-answer keys

The correct answer and short evidence are stored in every interactive item and appear after a choice. For audio, all answers follow the local transcript exactly. Open speaking and writing are assessed with the visible checklist, not automatic marking.
